OUR STORY

About Frame Wala

Frame Wala makes custom photo frames and personalized gifts for people who want a memory kept somewhere they'll actually see it — on a wall, a desk, or wrapped up as a gift for someone else. Every order is handled personally, from your first message to us to the day it reaches your door.
